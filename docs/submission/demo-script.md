# Demo recording plan — human presenter, target 3:40

**Not a video or completed execution record.** The narrative below is a
recording script. Conditional scenes must wait for the corresponding real
evidence; never stage a success or substitute local receipts under a Sepolia
label. [Rules and remaining gates](rules-and-gates.md) govern publication.

## Before recording

- Resolve the [existing live checklist](../todo-2026-09-11.md), then choose a
  reviewed baseline. Do not reset the existing market or reuse an expired run.
- Use desktop capture at 1920×1080 if available. Hide browser account menus,
  notifications, private tabs, account email, session URLs, raw signatures and
  provider resources. Show only the public proof export, never private journals.
- Prepare visible chain/trust/source labels, the original BLOCK and a new
  independently reviewed ALLOW. Use the actual displayed recommendation; 79.42%
  is an expected seed result, not a fixed live promise.
- The participant reads the risk decision and performs human review. Agent
  clicks, node signatures and chat approval cannot stand in for that step.
- Record normal-speed human speech. Edit only genuine waiting out of the same
  recorded workflow and label the cut; keep intent/transaction hashes
  consistent. The target timings below are budgets, not a measured voice
  recording.

## Screen plan and narration

### 0:00–0:20 — The operational problem

**画面：**产品名、当前参数变更区域；不超过一句背景。

> A multisig tells a protocol who can change a parameter. It does not tell the
> team whether that change is safe for the positions that exist now. ParamShield
> connects the reviewed risk evidence to the exact transaction that may execute.

### 0:20–0:55 — Live input and a dangerous proposal

**画面：**真实 hosted Graph 来源、区块、LT 80%→70%、四格风险；不要先弹待签交易。

> This is our reference lending market. I am proposing a lower liquidation
> threshold. The Graph supplies indexed positions and configuration at one
> block, which we corroborate against RPC. We compare the old and proposed
> threshold at normal prices and under a fifteen-percent price shock. Existing
> stressed exposure is separated from the extra exposure caused by this change.

### 0:55–1:30 — BLOCK and a new alternative

**画面：**BLOCK、规则、实际推荐值；点击推荐后显示新的分析/nonce。

> The confidential workflow blocks this proposal and searches for the closest
> passing decrease. We use the actual CRE CLI simulation lane and a trusted
> relay; this is not hardware-attested network execution. The rejected intent
> stays rejected. Choosing the computed candidate creates a new analysis and
> review. ALLOW means this change passes our model and policy, not that the
> entire market is safe.

### 1:30–2:00 — Source-grounded explanation

**画面：**询问风险、打开对应 source 字段、显示真实 AI／fallback 状态。

**Only after real AI acceptance:**

> The explanation selects facts already present in the evidence. Each numeric
> statement links back to its source. The AI cannot calculate an executable
> parameter, change the verdict, approve or send a transaction.

**If still unavailable, say instead:**

> The runtime model is not configured here. This is the explicitly labeled
> deterministic fallback, not an AI response. The evidence remains available,
> but our live AI acceptance gate is still open.

Fallback footage must not be used to imply The Graph AI category is complete.

### 2:00–3:10 — Human review and controlled execution

**仅在真实闭环完成时使用。画面与操作者：**

1. Account 2 审阅当前证据并签署精确授权。
2. 控制台“提交已审核提案”：Privy operator 发送，不等待 MetaMask。
3. Account 1（authority）审阅并发送决策交易。
4. Privy operator 受控执行；显示规范回执和最终 LT/stateVersion。

> I review this specific evidence and authorize the exact change. The Privy
> operator submits the proposal, while a separate authority records the
> decision. Privy signs only the allowed transaction and returns to DENY before
> broadcast. State-version, permission-epoch and expiry checks still apply at
> execution. We verify the mined payload, events and final state before
> reporting success.

If unfinished, record the real current stage and state the remaining gate. Do
not narrate the paragraph above over a pending receipt or an Anvil run.

### 3:10–3:40 — Proof and closing

**仅在真实 AFTER 已验证时：**打开公开 evidence、真实交易链接和 Graph 新事件检查。

> The final proof ties the proposal, policy decision, transaction receipts and
> after-state together. The hosted index now reflects the new parameter, which
> changes the analysis. Refreshing restores our records without renewing the
> authorization or sending another transaction. ParamShield turns a parameter
> change from blind signing into evidence-backed, policy-bound execution.

## Three short judge answers to rehearse

- **Why not a dashboard?** Our result gates the exact calldata and becomes
  invalid if the reviewed risk state or permissions change.
- **Why not let AI choose and execute?** Numerical decisions stay deterministic;
  AI has no authorization capability and only selects existing evidence facts.
- **What is trusted?** The single-host relay, decision authority and governance;
  CLI simulation is not a cryptographic proof of confidential network execution.
